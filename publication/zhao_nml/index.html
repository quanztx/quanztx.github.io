A Broad Range Triboelectric Stiffness Sensor for Variable Inclusions Recognition

Ziyi Zhao†, Zhentan Quan†, Huaze Tang†, Qinghao Xu, Hongfa Zhao, Zihan Wang, Ziwu Song, Shoujie Li, Ishara Dharmasena, Changsheng Wu, Wenbo Ding

† Equal contribution

Nano-Micro Letters 15, Article 233 · 2023
DOI: 10.1007/s40820-023-01201-7

Research overview

A self-powered triboelectric stiffness sensor decouples force and displacement signals and applies signal processing and SVM classification to recognize inclusions in soft objects.
