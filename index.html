Tsinghua University / Academic profile

Zhentan Quan 全振潭

Materials, sensing
& intelligent systems.

I am a Ph.D. student in Materials Science and Engineering at Tsinghua University, with backgrounds in electronic information and mechanical engineering.

My research experience spans tactile sensing for robotics, self-powered systems and near-sensor computing. I am interested in connecting materials and sensing with useful contact information for intelligent systems.

01

Research interests

Across materials, sensors and intelligent systems.

01 / CONTACT

Tactile sensing for robotics

Triboelectric stiffness sensing and visuo-tactile terrain identification: extracting useful contact information from sensor signals for robotic perception.

Tactile perception · Signal processing · Machine learning

02 / ENERGY

Self-powered sensing & energy harvesting

Triboelectric systems for low-frequency wave energy harvesting and deformation sensing, connecting mechanical design with electrical measurement.

Triboelectric systems · Mechanical design · Data acquisition

03 / MATERIALS

Materials & near-sensor computing

Origami sensing structures and two-dimensional materials for self-powered deformation monitoring and computation close to the sensor.

Responsive materials · Deformation monitoring · Edge computing
